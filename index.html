about me projects blog

Hi there, my name is Ambrose Ling, studying Computer Engineering at the University of Toronto. Some things I find pretty cool are large scale ML systems, computer architecture, accelerators, deep learning.

In the past I have done some startup work for LINKUPSS that develops video communication software for seniors during the pandemic, recognized by Google. I worked as a founding engineer at a productivity app startup (aSocial. inc), helping raised capital from investors and deployed to thousands of users.

I was a research intern at Professor Qian Lin's neuroscience lab where I developed zebra-fish locomotion prediction and tracking software using deep learning models.

I was a ML intern at Huawei where I worked on quantization, distillation of Image and Diffusion Models to edge devices.

Outside of school, I've been a contributor to the UTMIST (University of Toronto Student Team) community for almost 5 years where I was involved with leading engineering projects, technical writing and infrastructure development.

In my free time, you can find me going to the gym, hiking, playing some casual chess!

Feel free to connect with me through channels below if you would like to chat more! I'm always happy to meet new people
